# Console preferences

- Keep the Mission and Sonar displays free of the repeated "TX NOT COMMISSIONED" and "RX NOT COMMISSIONED" chips. The user explicitly requested their removal and does not want to repeat this preference.
- The Mission sonar card and Sonar workspace should use the same available C reference / acoustic preview as Validation, including configuration inspection before output starts. Physical receive samples or echo bins take priority when present.
- Preserve the distinction between reference previews and physical telemetry in data, recording and concise source descriptions. Removing a status chip must not fabricate hardware status or measurements.
- Keep the mounted sensor/TFT firmware and wiring intact. Do not flash the adaptive image without new user authorization and compatible bench wiring.
- Fill the acoustic preview chart at startup from the first reference return, then replace the seeded columns with new generated returns. Keep physical ping history unseeded.
- When battery readings are absent, use the separate battery scenario with assumed starting charge and deterministic load fluctuations. Present it as a battery scenario and never overwrite sensor/power telemetry or label it measured.
- Hide the policy qualification badge in Mission's Environment entry. Preserve qualification checks in the actual controls. Keep desktop Mission scroll-locked and fit its cards between the navigation and footer; retain scrolling in other workspaces and on narrow screens. Reserve model zoom for Alt + scroll or the assembly inspector. Preserve the card design and column layout.
- The interactive payload model must follow the seven real prototype photos in `docs/reference/prototype`, with the component map in `docs/PROTOTYPE_MODEL.md`. Keep its long straight acrylic tube, transparent flat plates and three physical bays. Do not restore the previous metal-capped concept model or add unphotographed under-body piezos. Keep the default assembly at 0%; regenerate GLB, manifest and hash together using `npm run model:build`.
- The payload is assembled and mounted and cannot be opened. Never ask for rewiring or disconnecting sensors/TFT/HC-SR04. Resolve pin or peripheral conflicts in firmware (the combined `arduino/05_sensors_adaptive_tx` image runs the adaptive engine inside the sensor sketch). The TDS board on A0 is the physical environment input; it is a TDS proxy, never labeled turbidity. Still do not flash without new authorization.
- Anything the board samples from its own DAC pin is an **MCU ADC CAPTURE**. Never label it measured PA4, oscilloscope, spectrum analyzer or instrument measurement, and derive no distortion figure from it. Those labels are reserved for an imported external capture. Keep measured, calculated and MCU-captured data visibly separate.
- MCU ADC captures carry a `tap` (RAW_DAC, AFE_FILTER, AFE_OUTPUT). Label the stage ones "MCU ADC capture · filter output / filtered output", never as the analog stage "measured". The ADC pin may only ever be fed from the raw DAC or from `ADC_IN` (biased 1.65 V, 1 kΩ, BAT54S clamp); never OPAMP_OUT or SCOPE_OUT directly. The bridge refuses a capture whose predicted pin range leaves 0.10 to 3.20 V.
- Build 6 (`arduino/05_sensors_adaptive_tx/build6`, SHA-256 7210091b…eca5) is the frozen firmware. Do not flash a new image unless a demonstrated bug requires it, and never without per-build authorization. The analog stage is not built; do not ask for it until the parts exist, and keep every analog, scope, power and acoustic result PENDING until a real record is imported.
